⚠️ In any real emergency, call 911 directly

TotaVivo's fall detection and 911 Find-Me Beacon are supplemental tools designed to help — they are not a certified medical alert system, are not monitored by any emergency response service, and are not guaranteed to work every time. Never rely on TotaVivo as your only way to get help.

1. TotaVivo is not a medical device

TotaVivo is a consumer convenience and accessibility app. It is not FDA-cleared or approved, has not been evaluated by any regulatory medical body, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Nothing in the app should be interpreted as a substitute for professional medical judgment. Always consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist for medical decisions.

2. Fall detection has real limits

Fall detection relies on your phone's motion sensor and a general movement pattern. It can miss real falls (a "false negative") and can also trigger with no fall at all (a "false positive"). Accuracy depends on your specific phone, where you carry it, your movement style, and conditions outside TotaVivo's control. It is a supplemental aid, not a guarantee that help will be summoned if you fall.

3. The 911 Find-Me Beacon and Personal Safety Alarm have real limits

The beacon and the Personal Safety Alarm (Vivo Guardian) sound a siren and flash lights to help people locate you and to deter a threat — they do not themselves contact 911 or any emergency service, and TotaVivo never automatically dials 911. When you use the alarm, TotaVivo can open your phone's own dialer with 911 entered, but you (or someone near you) must press Call, and connecting depends on your phone and signal. The Personal Safety Alarm is a self-activated helper for a capable person — it is not a monitored medical-alert or panic-button service, and no operator is watching over you. Siren loudness depends on your phone's volume and mute switch, and vibration is not available on all phones (for example iPhones). All of it depends on your phone having battery, being on and unlocked, and working normally. Automatic dialing and 24/7 monitored dispatch are planned for a future App Store version and are not active today.

4. TotaVivo is only as good as the phone it runs on

TotaVivo relies entirely on your phone's built-in sensors — accelerometer, GPS, camera, microphone, and battery reporting. If TotaVivo is installed on a device that lacks these sensors, or whose sensors are older, damaged, or less capable, the features that depend on them (fall detection, step counting, the Find-Me Beacon, battery reminders, and others) will not function as designed — or may not function at all. TotaVivo is software: it cannot add, repair, or upgrade any sensor your device does not already have. Before relying on any safety feature, test it on your actual phone.

5. Medication features are informational only

Medication reminders, barcode lookups, and adherence tracking are organizational tools. They are not a pharmacy, do not verify dosing against your specific prescriptions or health conditions, and are not a substitute for instructions from your doctor or pharmacist.

6. Sending and receiving money — TotaVivo is not a bank

The messaging screen's Send $ and Request $ options only make it easier to use payment channels you already have (such as your own bank, card, or payment app) — for example between a parent and an adult child. TotaVivo itself does not hold, transfer, or process funds, is not a bank or money-services business, does not charge for this convenience, and is not a party to any transaction. Any transfer happens entirely through the outside service you choose, under that service's own terms, protections, and fees.
