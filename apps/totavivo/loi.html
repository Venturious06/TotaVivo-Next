1. IMPORTANT — Assistant and Live Demo, NOT a Medical Device or Emergency Service

Please read this first and carefully. TotaVivo is currently a live demonstration and a personal-safety and daily-living ASSISTANT. It is NOT a certified medical device, NOT a monitored medical-alert service, NOT a 24/7 monitoring service, and NOT a replacement for 911, emergency responders, doctors, or caregivers. Some features work fully on the device today (for example, the safety siren, flashing lights, vibration, magnifier, and location); others are simulated in the demo (for example, real inbox, phone/911 dialing, cross-device sync, and banking) and only become real in a later App Store or backend version. The Participant agrees to KEEP every existing safety system, medical-alert service, caregiver arrangement, medication routine, and emergency plan fully in place, and to NOT rely on TotaVivo as their only means of safety, medical response, or communication. TotaVivo is used at the Participant's own discretion, and the Provider makes no medical or safety guarantee.

2. Non-Binding — No Cost — Stop Anytime

This Letter is completely non-binding. It creates NO legal obligation, NO financial obligation, and NO commitment to buy, subscribe, sign a future agreement, or continue after the pilot. The pilot is 100% free: there is no fee, invoice, subscription, deposit, or payment of any kind, now or later. Either party may pause, shorten, end the pilot, or withdraw this Letter at ANY time, for any reason or no reason, simply by telling the other — with no cost, penalty, notice period, or further obligation to anyone.

3. Purpose

The Participant is interested in trying TotaVivo during a free pilot to experience it in real daily use and share honest feedback. The goal is simply to learn together whether TotaVivo is helpful. Signing shows interest only — it does not obligate anyone to buy, subscribe, or continue.

5. What the Participant Provides

During the pilot, the Participant agrees to (a) use TotaVivo in a reasonable, good-faith way; (b) share candid feedback on what works and what does not; and (c) name a point of contact for questions and scheduling. 6. What TotaVivo Provides

During the pilot, the Provider will give the Participant free access to TotaVivo, along with reasonable setup help and support, at no charge. There is no fee, invoice, subscription, or payment of any kind connected to this pilot.
