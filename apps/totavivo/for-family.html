Safety, done honestly

Built to help in an emergency.

  • 📍
    Location for emergencies. If they ever fall or need help, TotaVivo can share their actual location with a caregiver or 911 — so responders know exactly where to go, even if your parent can't speak. It's never shared otherwise.
  • 🤸
    Automatic fall detection. Uses the phone's motion sensors to notice a hard fall and start the emergency check-in.
  • 🔒
    Private by default. No account required to try it. Nothing is shared without a clear, on-screen "yes."
Straight talk: TotaVivo is a helpful assistant, not a certified medical device — it's one more layer of safety, not a replacement for a monitored medical-alert service or emergency care.
