Questions about this policy? Contact us at privacy@totavivo.com.

2. What Data TotaVivo Collects — And Where It Lives

Local-first data

Information you enter into TotaVivo can include:

  • Contacts and phone favorites
  • Medications, dosages, and reminders
  • Calendar appointments
  • Health notes and adherence records
  • Smart home device settings
  • Earn activity and balance records
  • Personal preferences and accessibility settings

This information begins in your browser's on-device storage. If you do not enable cross-device sync, it remains local except when you deliberately use an external feature or provider. If you enable sync, the categories supported by the sync feature, including household contacts, medications, and selected activity, are stored in Supabase and shared only with authorized household members under database access rules.

Clearing browser data or uninstalling removes the local copy. It does not automatically delete information previously synced to Supabase; contact privacy@totavivo.com for assistance deleting a synced account or household.

Anonymous usage analytics and crash reports

TotaVivo uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate visit counts and Supabase for limited operational analytics and sanitized crash reports. TotaVivo does not place names, email addresses, phone numbers, messages, medication details, financial values, precise location, passwords, or authentication tokens in operational analytics. Reports use a randomly generated installation identifier, application version, screen identifier, connectivity status, and limited technical details. If the device is offline, reports wait in a capped on-device queue and are sent after connectivity returns.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not place names, email addresses, phone numbers, messages, medications, financial values, or precise location in operational analytics
  • Supabase receives an email address when you voluntarily create a passwordless sync account
  • Infrastructure providers may process IP addresses as necessary to deliver and secure their services
  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels
  • We do not sell any data — ever
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing

3. Device Permissions

TotaVivo may request the following device permissions. Each is used only for the specific feature described and only when you actively use that feature:

  • Camera / microphone — for video calls and the barcode/medication scanner. Never recorded or stored by TotaVivo.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer) — for fall detection. Processed entirely on-device. Not transmitted.
  • Location (geolocation) — for the 911 Find-Me Beacon to share your location with emergency services. Not stored or transmitted to TotaVivo.
  • Notifications — for medication reminders and fall alerts. Controlled entirely by you in your device settings.
  • Bluetooth — for pairing health devices (blood pressure monitors, glucometers, etc.). Not transmitted to TotaVivo.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission disables only that specific feature.

4. The Earn Feature

TotaVivo includes an optional Earn screen where users can participate in surveys, quizzes, and AI training tasks to earn small rewards. Important notes:

  • Participation is entirely opt-in — you choose each task individually
  • Earn tasks are currently illustrative/demo only — no real money changes hands during the beta period
  • When real Earn partners are integrated, their individual privacy practices will be disclosed at the time of each task
  • The "Train AI" tasks use anonymous, opt-in voice samples and text responses — never linked to your identity
  • A caregiver designated by you may view your Earn balance but cannot withdraw funds

5. Caregiver Access

TotaVivo includes optional caregiver viewing features. If you grant caregiver access:

  • The caregiver can view your Earn balance and activity summary
  • Caregivers cannot view your private messages, call history, or health notes
  • Caregivers cannot withdraw funds or make changes on your behalf
  • You control caregiver access and can revoke it at any time

6. HIPAA Notice

TotaVivo is not currently offered as a HIPAA-covered clinical record system or as a business-associate service. Optional cross-device sync can transmit information such as medication entries to Supabase. Do not use TotaVivo to store regulated patient records on behalf of a healthcare provider unless an appropriate compliance program, contracts, security review, and required business-associate agreements are in place.

If you are a healthcare provider testing TotaVivo: please do not enter real patient information. The demo uses fictional data. TotaVivo is not intended for clinical record-keeping.

7. Children's Privacy

TotaVivo is designed for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used TotaVivo, please contact us at privacy@totavivo.com.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Most TotaVivo data begins on your device. Optional cross-device sync and technical reliability reporting use Supabase:

  • To delete all TotaVivo data: clear your browser's site data for totavivo.com, or uninstall the app from your home screen
  • If you enable cross-device sync, synced household information is stored in Supabase so authorized household members can use it across devices
  • Sanitized operational analytics and crash reports may be retained for reliability and product-improvement purposes
  • Anonymous analytics data (visit counts) is retained by Cloudflare per their standard retention policy (90 days)

9. Third-Party Services

TotaVivo integrates or links to the following external services. Each governs its own data:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, CDN, and analytics. Cloudflare Privacy Policy →
  • Netlify — web hosting. Netlify Privacy Policy →
  • Supabase — optional account, cross-device sync, sanitized operational analytics, and crash reporting. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • IFTTT — smart home automations (if you connect). IFTTT Terms →
  • FDA drug database — queried for medication lookups. No personal data sent. Public API.

When TotaVivo links to external services (FaceTime, Zoom, Gmail, Chase, etc.), those services operate under their own privacy policies. TotaVivo does not share your data with them.

10. Security

TotaVivo takes the following security measures:

  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS (TLS 1.3)
  • Passwordless email authentication is used when you voluntarily enable Supabase cross-device sync
  • The application remains local-first; server-side processing is limited to enabled sync, account, subscription, analytics, and reliability services
  • Service worker caches only app files, not personal data

No system is 100% secure. If you discover a security issue, please report it to security@totavivo.com.
