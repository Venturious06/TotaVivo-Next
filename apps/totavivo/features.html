🧭 Your Personal Assistant & Aid

TotaVivo is designed to be the first thing you see and the place you start from — not just one more app to remember.

  • It's built to be your home base: put it on your home screen as the one you open first, and reach everything else — your other apps, your contacts, your calendar — through it.
  • Your Apps Hub lets you open your other apps right from inside TotaVivo, so you don't have to go hunting across your phone for what you need.
  • Everything you set up — your name, your favorites, your medications — is saved on your phone and picks up right where you left off the moment you reopen it, with nothing to reconfigure.

🔒 Your privacy is safe — no HIPAA worries

This is a demonstration built with made-up sample information ("Dorothy" and all her data are fictional). TotaVivo keeps everything on the device in your hand — no server, no account, no central database. Because no real patient information is created or shared, there's nothing here that raises HIPAA concerns. Please don't enter real patient details while testing.

👋 Let's Get to Know Each Other

TotaVivo learns your name, and uses it everywhere it speaks to you.
  • Tap the TotaVivo shield at the top of the app anytime → opens your Personal Safety Alarm (see Safety, below). Your name for greetings and reminders is set in the first-time setup and in Settings.
  • Optional real sync across devices — link with just an email (no password) so a caregiver's phone sees the same contacts, medications, and activity as yours, in real time. Everything else in TotaVivo still works completely free, offline, and with no account if you never turn this on.

🔎 Search & Home v7.3.4

Use it like any modern phone — search finds anything fast.
  • "Search Using TotaVivo" gold bar at the top of every screen — searches inside TotaVivo first (screens, contacts, medicines, apps), then the web/device. Tap it and the box grows for easy typing.
  • Time & date on the top line, a daily summary, live money tiles, weather, step count, and one-tap shortcuts. The time/battery strip is now optional — if your phone already shows them at the very top of its screen, turn TotaVivo's copy off in Settings → Display Mode and save the space.
  • Turn your phone sideways NEW — TotaVivo now rotates with your device and fills the screen edge-to-edge in both portrait and landscape, and the app always stays put inside the screen (pinch-to-zoom still works).
  • One close button everywhere — every pop-up page has the same small red ✕ in the top corner, same size on every screen, with a generous invisible tap area around it for shaky hands.
  • Inactivity alert & unusual-charge alert right on the Home screen — the things a caregiver most needs to notice, front and center.

🆘 Safety — the heart of TotaVivo

  • 🛡️ Personal Safety Alarm (Vivo Guardian) NEW — a deliberate panic/duress alarm for when you feel unsafe out in public, separate from fall detection. Tap the TotaVivo shield, then press & hold the big button: a loud siren, flashing lights and (on Android) vibration start instantly, with a 15-second "I'm Safe" cancel. Then big buttons to call 911 yourself and text your family your location. Honest by design — it opens your phone's dialer for 911 (it never secretly calls for you), and there's a Practice mode. Always free.
  • 911 Find-Me Beacon — turns on bright flashing red/yellow/blue lights and a loud alarm at full brightness and volume so help can find the person, inside or outside. Silence the sound anytime while the lights keep flashing.
  • Fall detection — uses the phone's motion sensor; a full-screen "Are you okay?" with countdown, then an escalation plan (your contacts in order, then 911). Saying or tapping "I'm okay" stops everything instantly.
  • Adjustable fall sensitivity NEW — choose Low, Medium, or High in Settings so simply setting the phone down doesn't trigger a false alarm. Having one of those days? Pause fall alerts until tomorrow with one tap — they turn back on by themselves, and step counting keeps working the whole time.
  • Pre-recorded messages played to responders, and a smart-home response (lights on, door unlocked for help).

💊 Health & Medications

  • Scan a medicine bottle barcode — TotaVivo looks the drug up in the FDA database and can add it for you in one tap.
  • Universal barcode & QR reader — reads any code and explains it aloud in plain English.
  • Medication reminders, refills, and adherence tracking; Quest, Labcorp, MyChart, Medicare available as one-tap apps.

♿ Made for older hands and eyes

  • Steady Touch — ignores accidental double-taps from shaky hands, with a practice pad to tune it.
  • Magnifier & read-aloud — magnify and have any printed text read out loud.
  • Voice Settings — choose the voice, speed, pitch, and volume; everything in the app can be read aloud. (In the app: Settings → Voice Settings, or just type "voice" in the search bar.)
  • Big Text — one tap in Settings → Accessibility makes text throughout the whole app larger, not just one screen.
  • Color-vision filters and gentle confirmation delays before anything happens.
  • All accessibility controls live together in Settings — nothing scattered across the app to hunt for.

📞 Everyday essentials

  • Phone with one-tap favorites you choose yourself, video calls (FaceTime/Zoom/Meet/WhatsApp), and a big dial pad.
  • Dialed an unknown number? One tap saves it as a new contact — no need to remember it or write it down.
  • Edit or add contacts right in the app — fix a number, add a relationship, or remove someone, with a 5-second undo if you change your mind.
  • Email, Messages, Calendar, Contacts — with plain-English summaries and read-aloud.
  • Bills — see what's due and pay in one tap; Bank & Checking views (a caregiver can see money but cannot withdraw).
  • Smart Home scenes & devices, Bluetooth health devices (hearing aids, BP monitor, pillbox, fall pendant), and automations — all tucked into Settings so the Home screen stays simple.

💰 Earn — get a little back for everyday use

A completely optional way for the person (or their family) to earn small rewards just by using their phone — modeled on today's popular "earn-to-use" reward phones, built for seniors.
  • Daily check-in streaks, quick surveys, brain quizzes, watching a short offer, a walking goal, and a friend-referral bonus.
  • 🧠 Train AI (pays more) — read a sentence aloud, pick the more helpful of two AI answers, answer in your own words, or describe a picture. AI companies pay for human feedback, and senior voices & opinions are in high demand. Opt-in and anonymous.
  • Optional 2% cashback on bills paid through TotaVivo, plus set-and-forget passive options.
  • An Earnings & Withdrawals Statement that builds up and is never reset — every payout is recorded with a running balance; cash out to Chase, PayPal, Zelle, or Apple Pay.

💵 Who actually pays?

The rewards don't come from the senior — they come from companies that already spend this money elsewhere:

  • Advertisers & brands pay to show a short ad or sponsored offer.
  • Market-research & survey companies pay for opinions (the surveys and quizzes).
  • AI companies pay for human feedback to train and improve their models — rating answers, voice samples, and short written replies (the "Train AI" tasks).
  • Cashback & affiliate partners pay a small commission when you shop or pay a bill through a partner — TotaVivo shares it back with you.
  • Opt-in anonymous research panels pay for aggregated, anonymized trends — never your name, contacts, or financial details, and only if you turn it on.
  • Referral bonuses come from TotaVivo's own sign-up budget (money it would otherwise spend on advertising).

It's private and your choice. You pick what to take part in; nothing personal or financial is ever sold without your OK, and a caregiver can see earnings but can't withdraw them. In this demo the amounts are illustrative — they show how it works; no real money changes hands during testing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family

Everyone who matters, not just one caregiver.
  • Your whole family list — tap to call or video call anyone, see who's close by, and a shared family group chat.
  • Recent activity feed — what your caregiver sees: medications taken, calls made, bills paid. Becomes real, live data once you turn on sync — otherwise shows what it will look like.
  • A caregiver can always see enough to know you're okay, and never enough to take over — they can't withdraw money, read private messages, or change your settings.

📊 Insights, 🧩 Simplify & 📥 Import

  • Insights — a plain-English report of how the app is used (most-used features, medication adherence, response times).
  • Features & Footprint — turn off any features you don't use to keep things simple and save memory; block apps you'll never need.
  • Import from your phone — choose what to bring in (contacts, favorites, email, meds, calendar, photos), and set how long records are kept.
