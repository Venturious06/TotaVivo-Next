🛡️ Tips When Reporting a Claim
State only what you directly saw, heard, and experienced. Avoid guessing about fault.
Record the date, time, location, weather, road conditions, vehicles, witnesses, and responding agency.
Photograph damage and the full scene before vehicles move, when it is safe and lawful.
Keep receipts, towing records, medical documents, claim numbers, and names of everyone you speak with.
Do not share Social Security numbers, banking passwords, or one-time security codes with an unsolicited caller.
TotaVivo helps organize information and contact assistance. It does not determine fault, provide legal advice, guarantee insurance coverage, or submit a claim without your review.
